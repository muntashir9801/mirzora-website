FAQ

Common Questions

Can you guarantee #1 ranking on Google?+

No. Local search visibility depends on several factors including relevance, distance and prominence. We focus on improving the accuracy, completeness and presentation of your business information.

Do I need a website if I already have Google Business Profile?+

A website isn't mandatory for every business, but a dedicated website can give customers more information and provide a stronger destination for enquiries.

Do you create websites for mobile users?+

Yes. All landing pages are designed to work across mobile, tablet and desktop screens.

Can customers contact me through WhatsApp?+

Yes. WhatsApp and click-to-call buttons can be integrated into the website.

Do you provide monthly management?+

Yes. Monthly Google Business Profile support is available.

Can you work with businesses outside Kolkata?+

Yes. Remote projects are available across India.

Do I need to give you my Google password?+

No. Business owners should provide appropriate account access through Google's supported user-access system rather than sharing passwords.

How long does a landing page take?+

Typical delivery depends on scope and how quickly the required business content and assets are provided. The timeline will be confirmed before the project begins.
